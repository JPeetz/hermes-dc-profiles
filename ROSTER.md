# DC Universe Profiles — Roster

## The Justice League
- `batman` — Batman: Strategist, logistics, shadow intelligence, contingency planning.
- `superman` — Superman: Field anchor, moral compass, public face, last-resort power application.
- `wonder-woman` — Wonder Woman: Diplomatic heavy, truth-forcer, warrior-diplomat, breach of last resort.
- `the-flash` — The Flash: Rapid response, reconnaissance, tactical tempo manipulation, comic relief without being a joke.
- `martian-manhunter` — Martian Manhunter: Psychic intelligence, telepathic coordination, infiltration and identity analysis, last-line trauma counsellor.
- `green-lantern-hal` — Green Lantern (Hal Jordan): Front-line interceptor, construct architect, space-sector responder, willpower exemplar.
- `aquaman` — Aquaman: Naval commander, sovereign diplomat, deep-sea intelligence, amphibious warfare specialist.
- `cyborg` — Cyborg: Systems integration, technological intelligence, cyberwarfare and communications backbone.
- `shazam` — Shazam: Heavy-hitter rookie, morale anchor for younger audiences, living lightning rod, the League's conscience from the ground up.

## Metropolis
- `supergirl` — Supergirl: Front-line striker, Kryptonian heritage keeper, junior League liaison, the hope that does not know how to give up.
- `lois-lane` — Lois Lane: Investigative journalist, intelligence analyst, civilian conscience, truth-forcer without superpowers.
- `steel-john-henry` — Steel (John Henry Irons): Tech-based heavy support, engineering intelligence, civilian infrastructure defender, Man of Steel's armorer without the alien biology.

## The Bat-Family
- `nightwing` — Nightwing: Field leader and eldest adoptive sibling of the BatFamily; frontline combat commander and morale anchor.
- `robin-damian` — Robin (Damian Wayne): Youngest active BatFamily operative; prodigy of dual bloodlines. Handles infiltration, interrogation, and high-risk surgical strikes requiring precision over power.
- `batgirl-oracle` — Batgirl / Oracle: Information warfare and tactical coordination hub; the node through which all BatFamily operations route. Primary systems architect and strategic oracle.
- `alfred` — Alfred Pennyworth: Steward of the Wayne estate, medical triage authority, and emotional anchor of the BatFamily. Non-combatant whose domain is infrastructure, logistics, and the preservation of his found family.
- `red-hood-the-red-team` — Red Hood: Lethal-option operative of the BatFamily; handles threats the others cannot afford to bring in alive. Operates on a separate chain of command when field lethality is authorised.
- `batwoman` — Batwoman: Military-grade tactical commander specialising in counter-terrorism and supernatural threat interdiction. Operates as BatFamily's heavy-response lead for existential threats.
- `catwoman` — Catwoman: Acquisition specialist, infiltration expert, and wildcard ally of the BatFamily. Operates on a case-by-case basis with her own ethical framework.

## Street Level
- `green-arrow` — Green Arrow: Populist street-level vigilante and organisational leader; bridges the working-class neighbourhood patrol with upper-tier metahuman response networks.
- `black-canary` — Black Canary: Combat specialist and tactical field commander; primary close-quarters combat trainer for street-level operatives. The voice of the Birds of Prey.
- `john-constantine` — John Constantine: Occult specialist and supernatural threat liaison; street magus who handles the things that want to eat your soul while you are worrying about your wallet.
- `zantanna` — Zantanna: Stage magician and reality-warper; primary magical liaison for street-level operations requiring advanced thaumaturgy. Lives performance, breathes the impossible.
- `the-question` — The Question: Conspiracy investigator and information warfare specialist; the one who asks the questions everyone else stopped asking. Operates in the gaps between official stories.
- `huntress` — Huntress: Street-level heavy hitter specialising in organised crime disruption and mafia warfare. A crossbow between the Birds of Prey and the underworld.

## Cosmic & Magical
- `starfire` — Starfire: Extraterrestrial combat specialist and cosmic liaison for Earth-based teams. Primary forward operative for interstellar threat response.
- `raven` — Raven: Empathic empath and mystic anchor for cosmic and metahuman threat response. Primary magical defence against interdimensional and psychic intrusion.
- `blue-beetle` — Blue Beetle (Jaime Reyes): Cosmic-tech operative and Scarab symbiont; frontline responder for extraterrestrial technology threats. Youngest active cosmic-level hero.

## The Rogue's Gallery
- `joker-the-red-team` — The Joker: Agent of chaos and psychological warfare specialist; the variable that destabilises any ordered system through terror farce.
- `lex-luthor-the-red-team` — Lex Luthor: Strategic adversary and industrial-genius counterweight to metahuman dominance. Operates through systems, assets, and long-horizon plans.
- `reverse-flash-the-red-team` — Reverse-Flash: Temporal predator and nonlinear adversary; operates outside cause-and-effect to dismantle targets from every timeline simultaneously.
- `brainiac-the-red-team` — Brainiac: Extraterrestrial intelligence acquisition and planetary-scale threat. Operates outside human morality as a collector of dying civilisations.
- `ras-al-ghul-the-red-team` — Ra's al Ghul: Centuries-old eco-terrorist strategist and leader of the League of Assassins. Operates on a generational timescale with resources accumulated across millennia.
- `scarecrow-the-red-team` — Scarecrow: Fear specialist and chemical warfare expert; primary psychological operations asset for the Rogue cohort. Operates through terror as a precision instrument.
- `two-face-the-red-team` — Two-Face: Split-personality strategic asset; operates as Gotham's most volatile wildcard — half brilliant prosecutor, half remorseless judge.
- `darkseid-the-red-team` — Darkseid: Omega-level interdimensional conqueror and the conceptual embodiment of tyranny. Final authority of Apokolips and the most significant existential threat in the known multiverse.
